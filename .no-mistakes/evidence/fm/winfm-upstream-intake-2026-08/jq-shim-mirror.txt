=== GitLab merge mocks: jq re-exposed by wrapper, not symlink (the Windows-lane failure) ===

--- 1. The mechanism, modelled on Linux with a Chocolatey-style shim (a binary that resolves a sibling file beside itself) ---
$ real/jq          (shim in its own directory)
  {"state":"opened",...} | jq -r .state  ->  opened            exit=0
$ mirror-symlink/jq   (upstream: ln -sf "$JQ_BIN" fakebin/jq)
  {"state":"opened",...} | jq -r .state  ->  cannot locate target config   exit=1
$ mirror-wrapper/jq   (landed: printf #!/usr/bin/env bash\nexec "$JQ_BIN" "$@")
  {"state":"opened",...} | jq -r .state  ->  opened            exit=0

--- 2. The lane failure reproduced through the real test, on Linux, by making the host jq shim-shaped ---
$ FM_TEST_JQ_BIN=<shim-shaped jq> tests/fm-pr-merge.test.sh    # with upstream's ln -sf re-exposure
  not ok - gitlab-merges: a well-formed merge request URL should merge, not error: expected exit 0, got 1
   ^ byte-identical to the Windows lane's reported failure text

$ FM_TEST_JQ_BIN=<shim-shaped jq> tests/fm-pr-merge.test.sh    # with the landed wrapper re-exposure
  (every gitlab case passes)
